# U.S. tech layoffs in 2026

Snapshot as of November 18, 2026. The federal figures cover U.S. jobs through September. Challenger, a private outplacement firm, tracks announcements by U.S.-based employers through October. Company-wide disclosures can include roles outside the U.S.

| Measure | 2026 year to date | Same period in 2025 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Announced job cuts at U.S.-based technology employers, January–October. [Challenger](https://www.challengergray.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Challenger-Report-October-2026.pdf) | 171,501 | 141,159 |
| Estimated U.S. layoffs and discharges in the Information industry, January–September. [BLS via FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/JTU5100LDL) | 455,000 | 332,000 |
| Estimated U.S. layoffs and discharges across all nonfarm employers, January–September. [BLS via FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/JTULDL) | 15.41 million | 15.75 million |

The BLS survey includes firings and can capture cuts that never made the news. It counts separations by employer industry and does not collect occupations. The Information industry includes software publishing, telecom, and media. An IT worker at a hospital is counted in health care. [JOLTS FAQ](https://www.bls.gov/jlt/jltfaq.htm) · [BLS industry definition](https://www.bls.gov/iag/tgs/iag51.htm)

## Where to find cuts beyond the headlines

- **State WARN notices** record planned cuts at specific employers and worksites. Washington listed 93 Uber workers scheduled to separate on November 2 and 58 Expedia workers on November 21. Browse the official records for [California](https://edd.ca.gov/en/jobs_and_training/layoff_services_warn), [New York](https://dol.ny.gov/warn-dashboard), and [Washington](https://fortress.wa.gov/esd/file/WARN/Public/SearchWARN.aspx), or download the independently compiled [WARN Index dataset](https://warnindex.com/api-docs). Smaller rounds can fall below reporting thresholds. Company announcements and state notices can cover the same workers. [Washington economic report](https://ofm.wa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/October_2026_Economic_report.pdf) · [Federal WARN rules](https://webapps.dol.gov/elaws/elg/layoffs.htm)
- **Federal estimates** give a broader view. In September, about 73% of private-sector layoffs and discharges occurred at worksites with fewer than 250 employees, including smaller locations of large companies. There is no public 2026 count of unreported layoffs specifically in IT occupations. [BLS worksite-size breakdown](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/jolts.t07.htm) · [JOLTS FAQ](https://www.bls.gov/jlt/jltfaq.htm)
