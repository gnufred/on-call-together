# European tech layoffs in 2026

Snapshot as of November 18, 2026. The regional tally covers the EU and Norway. The UK has a separate national series.

## Large cuts announced in Information / Computing

| Eurofound country-level records, January–October | 2026 | 2025 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Planned job losses | **20,193** | **6,563** |
| Announcements with a published job-loss count | 61 | 28 |

The totals sum the minimum planned losses displayed in Eurofound's Information / Computing listings by announcement date. They exclude cross-border entries labelled `European Union` or `World` to avoid double counting country records. The sector includes telecom and software services and covers all job functions within the affected employers. [Eurofound sector listings](https://apps.eurofound.europa.eu/restructuring-events/sectors/Information_Computing) · [Eurofound methodology](https://apps.eurofound.europa.eu/restructuring-events/methodology)

Eurofound, an EU agency, generally records publicly reported events affecting at least 100 jobs or at least 10% of staff at sites with more than 250 employees. The figures can include voluntary departures and plans that take effect in later years. Coverage varies by country and how visible the cuts are. Eurostat publishes flows into and out of employment, but those flows do not isolate layoffs. A comparable Europe-wide count of completed 2026 layoffs in IT occupations is unavailable in these sources. [Eurofound methodology](https://apps.eurofound.europa.eu/restructuring-events/methodology) · [ECB analysis](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/economic-bulletin/focus/2026/html/ecb.ebbox202604_05~a606096644.en.html) · [Eurostat labour market flows](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20260911-3)

## National data beyond the headlines

| Country | Latest official figures available for this snapshot |
| --- | --- |
| United Kingdom | The ONS household survey estimated **120,000 redundancies** in July–September, including voluntary redundancies, versus **134,000** a year earlier. The year-over-year difference is within sampling variability. The ONS also publishes an industry breakdown and a separate series of potential cuts notified by employers in Great Britain. [ONS November bulletin](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/employmentandemployeetypes/bulletins/employmentintheuk/november2026) · [Industry breakdown](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peoplenotinwork/redundancies/datasets/redundanciesbyindustryagesexandreemploymentratesred02/current) · [Employer notices](https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/employmentandemployeetypes/datasets/hr1potentialredundancies) |
| France | DARES recorded **251,600 employer-initiated dismissals** in Q2, including **18,100 for economic reasons**. The series covers the private sector in metropolitan France, excluding agriculture, temporary agency work, and private household employers. [DARES dismissal statistics](https://dares.travail-emploi.gouv.fr/donnees/les-licenciements) |
| Norway | Employers notified NAV of potential mass dismissals covering **19,329 workers** from January to October, versus **15,220** over the same months of 2025. The 2026 total includes **459** in computer programming, consultancy and related activities. These are sums of the monthly notice figures. [NAV October tables, pages 1 and 25](https://www.nav.no/_/attachment/download/5582db20-b4c8-4501-a11d-1db0c52772aa%3A904d7245240dd0f72af2c321f551f49047dc7de6/202610_AG200%20Melding%20om%20permittering%20og%20masseoppsigelse.%20Maaned.pdf) · [NAV methodology](https://www.nav.no/no/nav-og-samfunn/statistikk/arbeidssokere-og-stillinger-statistikk/permitteringsvarsel%20og%20permitterte) |

EU rules require employers to notify the competent public authority of planned collective redundancies. National series measure different things, including actual dismissals, survey-reported redundancies, and advance notices. Their totals cannot be added to produce a Europe-wide count. [EU Collective Redundancies Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:31998L0059)
