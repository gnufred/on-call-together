# The French IT job market in the AI era

Evidence checked on **23 November 2026**. The sources below cover different years, populations and measures. The search decisions are interpretations of that evidence. Apec’s employment category covers managerial and professional staff, including specialists without direct reports. [Apec’s English-language description of the category](https://corporate.apec.fr/files/live/sites/corporate/files/Nos%20%C3%A9tudes/pdf/recruiting-managerial-and-professional-staff-in-europe)

## A slower hiring market can still have difficult-to-fill IT roles

| Measure | What the source found | How to use it |

**Search implication.** Apply where the work, hiring stage and location can be checked directly. A national figure aggregates quite different markets including software delivery, infrastructure, user support, enterprise systems, data, and security. In a tight search, keep the same evidence and widen the employer type first. Try software publishers, internal IT at an end-user organisation, regulated enterprises, consulting and IT services firms, and public-sector digital teams. Each is a separate search hypothesis to test with live postings. A consultancy search also needs to establish whether a client mission exists, where it is performed and whether the remote policy changes between assignments.

## Paris and Île-de-France

Apec recorded **136,350** managerial and professional hires in Île-de-France in 2025, down **5%**. Its spring forecast was **143,160** for 2026, up **5%**. That was about **47%** of projected national hires in the category across all functions. The size of the region makes it a productive place to start mapping employers. A Paris or Île-de-France headquarters tells you where to look for teams and contacts. The location of the team, contract, client site and required attendance are independent pieces of evidence. [Apec Île-de-France outlook](https://corporate.apec.fr/lemploi-cadre-en-ile-de-france.html), [national forecast by region](https://corporate.apec.fr/files/live/sites/corporate/files/Nos%20etudes/PDF/Previsions-de-recrutements-de-cadres-2026#page=5)

France Travail’s employer survey on hiring needs (BMO) gives a narrower local example. For the grouped occupation **IT engineers**, intended recruitment in Île-de-France fell from **19,770** projects in 2024 to **17,570** in 2025 and **14,390** in 2026. The 2026 plans included **6,170** in Hauts-de-Seine and **4,680** in Paris. These rounded project counts show why Paris and Hauts-de-Seine merit separate alerts, and why a forecast for the broader managerial and professional category can coexist with a weaker result in a particular occupation. France Travail uses the survey to record employers’ anticipated hiring projects, with geographic detail down to the local employment area. [BMO Île-de-France, grouped IT engineering occupations](https://statistiques.francetravail.org/bmo/bmo?fa=11&fd=M2X&lb=0&pp=2024-2026&ss=1), [BMO methodology](https://statistiques.francetravail.org/bmo/static/methode_2026)

Nationally, the same BMO vintage reports different numbers for the more detailed occupation entries. Use the table to find related types of work. Each row uses the survey’s occupational classification. Difficulty is the share of projects that surveyed employers anticipated would be hard to fill. Across all occupations, BMO reported about **2.3 million** potential hires (down **6.5%** from 2025) and expected difficulty in **43.8%** of projects. [France Travail, BMO 2026 by occupation](https://statistiques.francetravail.org/bmo/met?le=0&pp=2026&ss=1), [national BMO results](https://statistiques.francetravail.org/bmo/bmopub/228435)

| Selected BMO occupation | Projects | Anticipated difficulty |

Eurostat estimated about **1.4 million** ICT specialists employed in France in 2025 under its occupation-based definition. This large installed workforce is a reason to look outside technology-company job boards. Explore the teams responsible for systems, production, data or security inside organisations whose main business is elsewhere. Treat workforce stock, job-board advertisements, and new hiring projects as three different measures. ## Remote work, hybrid schedules and pay

Insee measured actual work behaviour over four weeks in 2025. About **20%** of salaried workers, **52%** of managerial and professional staff and **70%** of workers in information and communication teleworked at least one day per week on average. For that sector, **28%** worked remotely three days a week or more. This confirms that telework is established in that sector. The instrument groups three or more days together and offers no fully remote vacancy rate. Apec’s March 2026 survey also found policy varied by employer. **9%** of employers said they had reduced or removed authorised telework days for managerial and professional staff in 2025 and **2%** had increased them. [Insee, Working Time Arrangements, figure 3a](https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/8733073), [Apec, Employer and Employee Views on Remote Work](https://corporate.apec.fr/home/nos-etudes/toutes-nos-etudes/regard-des-cadres-et-employeurs-sur-le-teletravail.html)

For a search, check the work arrangement at the level of each role. Use the [France eligibility and attendance checklist](search.md#verify-remote-eligibility-and-office-cadence).

In Apec’s November 2026 pay study, the national medians for managerial and professional staff already in post were **€50k** for software development, **€55k** for infrastructure and systems, and **€60k** for information security. The central 80% ranges were **€38–74k**, **€41–85k**, and **€43–99k** respectively. The corresponding Île-de-France medians were **€55k**, **€62k**, and **€65k**. These are annual gross fixed plus variable compensation across broad job families, pooled across the 2022–2026 surveys. Read the family breakdown by geography, scope, sector and responsibility before comparing current vacancy ranges. Ask for fixed gross annual pay, variable target, and on-call compensation if applicable. Salary medians for people in post provide context for an offer discussion. [Apec, 2026 pay study, pp. 56, 60 and 64](https://corporate.apec.fr/files/live/sites/corporate/files/Nos%20etudes/PDF/Les%20remunerations%20des%20cadres%20dans%20111%20familles%20de%20metiers%20ed%202026.pdf#page=56), [survey scope](https://corporate.apec.fr/home/nos-etudes/toutes-nos-etudes/les-remunerations-des-cadres-dans-111-familles-de-metiers-edition-2026.html)

## What AI adoption data tells a candidate

| Evidence | Useful interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| **Business adoption.** Insee’s harmonised ICT survey reports that **18%** of in-scope French enterprises used at least one AI technology in 2025, up from **10%** in 2024. The EU figure was **20%** in 2025. In France, reported use was **59%** in information and communication, and **58%** among firms with at least 250 employees. The French survey covers employers of at least 10 employees in mainly market sectors outside agriculture, finance and insurance. [Insee ICT survey](https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/9025878?sommaire=8677764) | Target and ask about actual maturity. A firm that uses a single AI feature in one workflow and an organisation operating many AI-assisted systems both count as adopters. Financial-sector roles need their own evidence because of the survey’s scope. |
| **Use and obstacles.** In the same survey, **54%** of non-adopters cited a lack of expertise, while **71%** saw no use for AI in their business. Several reasons could be reported. [Insee ICT survey, figure 4](https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/9025878?sommaire=8677764) | Bring a concrete workflow and its operational limits to interviews. Be prepared to explain what the team would measure, how a human checks the result and when a normal software solution is cheaper or more predictable. |
| **AI terms in ads.** OECD’s 2026 analysis of Indeed data shows the French share of ads mentioning AI-related terms rising into late 2025. Indeed’s methodology counts mentions of terms such as *machine learning*, *data science* or *large language models* in the advert text. [OECD Employment Outlook 2026](https://www.oecd.org/fr/publications/perspectives-de-l-emploi-de-l-ocde-2026_f8a23d95-fr/full-report/component-5.html), [Indeed Hiring Lab methodology](https://hiringlab.indeed.com/fr/blog/2026/10/28/panorama-du-marche-du-travail-europeen-octobre-2026/) | A mention can mean the job builds AI, integrates AI, uses approved AI tools or is advertised by an AI business. Read the responsibilities to determine which. Platform share depends on both the number of AI-related ads and how other ads move. |

For evidence about recruiting workflows specifically, see [Apec’s findings on employers and job seekers](applications.md#what-employers-have-reported). Adoption, capability, labour demand, hires and headcount all answer different questions. The ILO’s 2025 task-based index estimates that about one in four workers globally are in occupations with some generative AI exposure, with a much smaller group in the highest band. A task-exposure score represents technical potential under an assumed set of tasks. Turning that score into staffing scenarios requires assumptions about use, economics, changes to workflows and new demand. Use the result to identify which tasks to practice and which skills to demonstrate. [ILO–NASK, occupational exposure index](https://www.ilo.org/publications/generative-ai-and-jobs-refined-global-index-occupational-exposure), [ILO, what exposure indicators measure](https://www.ilo.org/publications/workers%E2%80%99-exposure-ai-what-indicators-tell-us-%E2%80%93-and-what-they-don%E2%80%99t)

## The evidence on entry-level hiring and software productivity

The October 2026 LIFT working paper from researchers at HEC Paris and Banque de France used French administrative data through March 2026. Open-ended hires **and** separations fell more in occupations classified as most exposed to AI after November 2022, particularly for young entrants. The authors found a similar hiring pattern among firms reporting intensive use of AI, with no clear divergence in their total employment relative to other firms. The authors also discuss telework and the economic cycle as potentially overlapping influences. For an early-career search, this supports testing more entry routes and looking for teams that explicitly provide feedback, code or change review, and progression into greater responsibility. Ask what the last junior hire delivered after the first three months. [LIFT Working Paper 2026-02](https://heclift.github.io/website/en/pubs/exposition-ia-emploi/index.html), [Banque de France discussion and limitations](https://www.banque-france.fr/en/publications-and-statistics/publications/generative-ai-first-changes-labour-market-are-affecting-young-people)

A separate November 2026 indicator from Coface and OEM compared France Travail postings in occupations with different AI exposure within sectors. In Q3 2026, a **10-percentage-point** higher exposure score was associated with a **12.4%** weaker change in postings relative to the Q3 2022 baseline. The relative pattern also appeared in postings requiring experience. This is an observational comparison of one source of adverts. It is distinct from hires, separations and headcount. For an experienced search, keep an eye on verified role volume and be prepared to explain how you extend judgement or delivery capacity across a team. [Coface–OEM, AI-LERT methodology and results](https://www.coface.com/fr/actualites-economie-conseils-d-experts/ia-et-emploi-quatre-ans-apres-le-lancement-de-chatgpt-les-offres-d-emploi-des-metiers-les-plus-exposes-decrochent)

Software studies show why candidates should quantify their own workflows carefully. Cui and coauthors pooled three company field experiments involving **4,867 developers** with randomized access to a coding assistant. They estimated **26.08% more tasks completed** with tool use (standard error 10.3 percentage points). METR’s early-2025 experiment measured **time per task** on mature repositories where **16 experienced developers** already worked. Its AI-allowed tasks took **19% longer** on average. The later METR experiment reported possible speedups, with wide intervals and selection problems, including participants keeping some tasks out of the experiment. These studies use different tools, participants, task selections and outcomes. DORA’s software-delivery research also identifies team-level associations, including higher throughput and higher delivery instability alongside greater AI adoption in its 2025 study. [Cui et al., Management Science](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2025.00535), [METR trial](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09089), [METR follow-up and design limitations](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/), [DORA, effective software delivery with AI](https://dora.dev/insights/balancing-ai-tensions/)

**Interview evidence to collect.** Pick one real task type such as debugging a regression, drafting integration tests, documenting an incident or migrating a dependency. Record time through verification, what the tool misunderstood, the check that caught it, and which changes reached review or deployment. For support and operations, credible measures could be repeated tickets, reproducibility of a diagnosis, alert quality or time to recovery. If you lack a baseline, show a specific accepted change, a test, or a review decision. Keep the claim as narrow as the evidence.

## Refresh this picture while searching

- **Each week, inspect employer advertisements.** Count unique, still-open roles with a verified application destination. Track discipline, seniority, contract type, exact work site, required presence, working language, hard requirements, concrete AI responsibilities and whether AI appears only in the employer’s general description. Treat your sample as a description of the sources and filters you used.
- **Each quarter, refresh hiring conditions.** Use the [Apec research library](https://corporate.apec.fr/toutes-nos-etudes), [France Travail vacancy and recruitment publications](https://statistiques.francetravail.org/offres/offrespub?type=2), and [Insee economic dashboard](https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/2107840). Record both publication date and observation period.
- **Each year, refresh occupational and geographical baselines.** Use [France Travail BMO](https://statistiques.francetravail.org/bmo/), the Dares–France Travail tension series and Apec pay by family. For cyber role vocabulary, the [ANSSI–DGEFP–Afpa observatory](https://cyber.gouv.fr/offre-de-service/formations-entrainement-et-decouverte-des-metiers/observatoires-des-metiers/observatoire-des-metiers-enquete-2025/) analysed over 23,000 advertisements from June 2023 to June 2024 and published separate profiles. Use it to learn how roles are named and scoped, then confirm current openings with employers.
- **Change a search decision when the signal repeats.** If one role family yields very few viable ads, compare adjacent titles with overlapping responsibilities. If employer ads regularly show hybrid and the cadence is unclear, record the reply before treating them as matches. If your search consistently reaches interviews but stalls at the technical stage, use feedback and interview themes to decide what to practise next.
