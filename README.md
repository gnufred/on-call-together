## How to host a call

Choose any meeting service with live video and audio. Turn on your webcam and microphone. Share the invite link with #onCallTogether on your social network (I guess it's going to be LinkedIn).

Set a timer for 25–30 minutes. ⏱️

When you **host**, listen with respect and an open mind, without judgment. Give your guests room to share.
